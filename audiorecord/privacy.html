AudioRecord Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

AudioRecord records your microphone and your Mac's system audio into one file on your Mac. This policy explains, in plain terms, exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. AudioRecord collects no personal data, has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app works without an account or sign-in.

Your recordings

  • Recordings are processed entirely on your Mac and are stored only on your Mac, in the folder you choose.
  • Recordings never leave your device and are never uploaded by us.
  • You can delete any recording at any time from within the app or in Finder.

Network use

None. The app makes no network requests of its own. The only internet interaction is with the App Store, handled entirely by Apple, when you choose to purchase or restore the full version. No information about you or your recordings is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only to record your voice while you are recording, at your request.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording: used only to capture system audio (what you hear) while you are recording. The app never captures your screen contents or takes screenshots.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.
