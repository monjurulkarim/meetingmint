AudioRecord Support

Answers to common questions. Stuck? Email us — we reply fast.

The app is asking for "Screen & System Audio Recording" — why?

macOS delivers system audio (everything your Mac is playing) through ScreenCaptureKit, which Apple groups under the Screen Recording permission. AudioRecord uses it only for audio — it never captures your screen. After granting, macOS requires one app relaunch; AudioRecord shows an alert and relaunches itself for you.

The Microphone permission was denied. How do I turn it back on?

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, enable AudioRecord, then return to the app. (The app can only appear in that list after it has asked once.) If it still fails, toggle the switch off and on.

Where are my recordings?

In the folder shown at the top of the app's Library — by default ~/Documents/AudioRecord inside the app's container. You can pick any folder with Choose Save Folder…; the app remembers it across launches.

What's the difference between WAV and M4A?

WAV is lossless (32-bit float) — best for editing. M4A (AAC) is much smaller — best for sharing. Choose per recording before you hit Record.

How does the trial work?

Everything is free and fully functional for 7 days. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks recording forever — no subscription. Restore purchases from Settings → License on any Mac signed into the same Apple ID.

Recording sounds choppy on my USB microphone

Update to the latest version — the mixer now waits out burst-y USB/Bluetooth microphones instead of inserting silence. If you still hear gaps, email us your mic model and we'll investigate.
