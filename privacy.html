MeetingMint Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

MeetingMint is designed so that your meeting content stays on your Mac. This policy explains, in plain terms, exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. MeetingMint collects no personal data, has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app works without an account or sign-in.

Your audio and meetings

  • Audio recordings and transcripts are processed entirely on your Mac and are stored only on your Mac.
  • Audio and transcripts never leave your device and are never uploaded by us.
  • You can delete any meeting and its audio at any time from within the app.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only for a one-time, optional download of open-source AI models (~1.6–3.1 GB) when you choose to set them up, and to communicate with the App Store for purchases. No information about you or your meetings is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.
