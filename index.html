MeetingMint

On-device AI meeting notes for the Mac.

What is MeetingMint?

MeetingMint records your microphone or imports any audio file and turns it into clean meeting minutes — action items, decisions, and key points — using AI that runs entirely on your Mac. No cloud service, no account, and your audio never leaves your device.

Requirements: Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. A one-time download of open-source AI models (~1.6–3.1 GB, RAM-dependent) happens on first use over your internet connection; everything after that runs offline.

Privacy

  • Audio and transcripts never leave your Mac.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The only network use is the optional one-time model download.

Frequently asked questions

Does it record Zoom / Teams / Meet audio?

Version 1.0 records the microphone and imports audio files. To get minutes of an online meeting, use the meeting app's own "record meeting" feature, save the file, and import it into MeetingMint — that works with every platform.

Why does the first meeting take a while?

The first transcription downloads the AI models once. After that, everything runs locally and offline.

Can I use Bluetooth headphones?

Yes — including recording through Bluetooth headset microphones.

What formats can I export?

Minutes export to PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown, HTML, and plain text; the original audio can be exported as-is.
